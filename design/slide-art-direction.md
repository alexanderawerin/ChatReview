# Visual system for chat recaps

All moods use flat colour and typography. Illustrated backgrounds are retired, including on landing previews. Photo slides retain centered full-frame photography and a small author credit.

- Calm is a neutral business report: light surface, graphite text, factual language and no decorative emoji. Award facts use one table with consistent name/value columns. The title and explanation sit directly above the table; the winner is a row, not an oversized number.
- Friendly uses lilac, blue, mint and pink pastels, gentle humour and warm emoji. The overview is the first slide and its fact card is centered. Award cards read emoji, value/unit, then participant name; the other participants remain visible alongside it. Media rankings put a meaningful icon on each row, while the words emoji sits under the heading. Other rankings and quotes keep their emoji in the heading zone. Charts may omit it.
- Sharp leads with a biting, sarcastic headline and its own mocking or incredulous emoji. The evidence column follows without a dividing rule. The winner name and participant list align left. Very dark warm backgrounds use red, orange, amber and yellow accents. The joke has more visual weight than the number.

Facts, participant selection and source limitations stay consistent across moods. Decorative emoji differ for every corresponding friendly/roast slide. Source content, including reaction symbols and quoted messages, remains verbatim; mood styling does not rewrite it. Reaction coverage uses a plain-language note instead of the export field name. All moods show applicable timing, threshold and vocabulary-selection notes.

Comparable awards share type roles and reserve two name lines. Dates use Russian day-month-year labels; standalone captions omit terminal full stops. The landing carousel contains exactly three corresponding moods, reaches the viewport edges and aligns its navigation with the instruction content below. The instruction heading-to-description gap is 4px.

Validate at 1280×720 export size and desktop/Full HD viewing sizes. Physical television viewing distance and the user's visual acceptance remain separate from automated geometry checks. Existing illustration assets are retained in the repository but are no longer used as backgrounds.
